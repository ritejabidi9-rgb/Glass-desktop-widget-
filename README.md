 # Glass-desktop-widget-
Lightweight glass-style desktop widgets for Windows. Personalized, aesthetic, and easy to set up, in one standalone app with no extra software or complicated configuration.
## What you get

Glass Widgets gives your Windows desktop a set of soft, translucent widgets that blend with your wallpaper. Each one can be moved, resized, and restyled (colors, opacity, text) to match your own aesthetic, and they stay light on your PC.

- **Spotify widget:** a now-playing bar with album art, progress, and play / pause / next controls. No login or API key needed.
- **Weather widget:** temperature, daily high and low, and a clean drawn icon for your city. No account needed.
- **Classroom / Files widget:** this one is more than a Classroom viewer. Connect your Google Classroom and it shows your newest assignments, materials, and posts with files, and clicking an item opens it. Or, if you don't use Classroom, switch it to show your **recently opened files** instead (right-click and choose "Show recent files"), so it works as a quick-access list of whatever you were just working on.
## New to this? Start here

These widgets run on a free program called Python, so install that first from [python.org](https://www.python.org/downloads/) (tick **"Add Python to PATH"** during setup). Then click the green **Code** button on this page, choose **Download ZIP**, and unzip the folder anywhere on your PC. Open it and double-click **`install.bat`**. You only need to do this once, and it downloads everything the widgets need. After that, open the folder of the widget you want (**spotify**, **weather**, or **classroom**) and double-click **`run.bat`**. The widget appears on your desktop. Drag it to move it, drag its edges to resize it, and right-click it to change the colors. That's it!

📖 **Need more details?** Open the **README** inside each widget folder, or the full setup guide in the [`docs`](docs) folder (the Classroom widget needs a free Google key, explained step by step there).
